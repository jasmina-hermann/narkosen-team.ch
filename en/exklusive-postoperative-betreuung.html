03.

Exclusive postoperative care.

After surgery, comprehensive aftercare is essential for a quick and smooth recovery. Our experienced team offers you exclusive postoperative care, characterised by close monitoring, multimodal pain management and availability by phone after your discharge.

Close postoperative monitoring.

After your operation, your safety is our top priority. Our close postoperative monitoring ensures that all vital functions are continuously checked. This intensive care allows us to make sure that you are in the best medical hands and that your recovery goes as well as possible.

Multimodal, differentiated pain management.

A central part of our exclusive postoperative care is multimodal, differentiated pain management. We use a variety of approaches to relieve your pain in a targeted way while minimising side effects. We combine medication with non-drug methods to ensure individual pain treatment. Our goal is to restore your well-being, ensure a rapid recovery and enable you to return quickly to your usual activities.

Available by phone after discharge.

After you leave the clinic, we want to make sure you continue to receive the best possible care. Being available by phone after your discharge gives you the opportunity to contact us with any questions or concerns.

Our team is there for you and offers you the support you need. Your well-being is close to our hearts, and we want you to feel safe and well looked after even after you return home.
