Through the way we work and our ethical principles, we create synergies that benefit both the efficiency of your business and the safety and satisfaction of your patients.

practices.

You would like to perform diagnostic or surgical procedures under anaesthesia and

  • concentrate fully and without interruption on your core activity, operating
  • ensure that your patients receive the best possible care during surgery, to the highest safety standards, in a calm atmosphere
  • expect your anaesthesia partners to set themselves standards as high as your own
  • value continuity of staff, flexibility and professionalism

You are a cardiologist and are looking for an anaesthesia partner with:

  • many years of experience in cardiac anaesthesia and cardiac intensive care
  • in-depth, certified expertise in TEE diagnostics

Then we are delighted that you have found our website, because with narkose-team.ch GmbH you have already found the partner who can make all of this possible for you.

If you do not have a permanently equipped anaesthesia workstation, that is not a problem for us. Our mobile equipment allows us to set up a modern anaesthesia workstation in any operating room and any practice, regardless of the existing infrastructure, without compromising the quality of treatment or the safety of our patients. For regular assignments, we are happy to set up a dedicated workstation in your practice's operating room.

Depending on availability, we can also provide you with a theatre nurse.

outpatient surgical centres.

If you run a surgical centre and find it difficult to staff or efficiently manage your anaesthesia department, you have come to the right place. We offer tailor-made solutions for all your anaesthesia needs and help you ensure high-quality patient care.

Anaesthesia expertise for outpatient procedures.

Our team consists of highly qualified anaesthetists, nurses and surgical technologists with extensive experience in outpatient surgical and diagnostic procedures. Whether you need support for minor procedures or more complex ones, we are there for you with our expertise. Our expertise ensures that your patients receive optimal care throughout the entire procedure. Our anaesthesia team adapts the anaesthesia procedures individually to the needs of your patients.

Outsourcing your anaesthesia department.

If you would like to outsource your anaesthesia department to a competent partner, we are the right people to talk to. At narkose-team.ch we understand the challenges of staff planning and management in anaesthesia. Our goal is to offer you constructive solutions that take into account both your organisational and financial needs.

hospitals.

If you run a hospital and find it difficult to staff your anaesthesia department, or have a short-term need for medical or nursing anaesthesia staff, we are the ideal partner for you. Our tailor-made solutions help you ensure first-class patient care.

Flexible anaesthesia care.

In today's fast-paced healthcare, it is often challenging to find enough qualified staff. Our highly qualified anaesthetists and nurse anaesthetists are available to you at short notice. This enables you to resolve staff shortages in your anaesthesia department quickly and efficiently.

Our specialists bring extensive experience in performing anaesthesia procedures across various surgical disciplines. They are fully familiar with the latest technologies and methods to provide your patients with safe and comfortable anaesthesia.

We understand that every hospital has individual needs. That is why we offer tailor-made solutions that are adapted to your specific requirements.
